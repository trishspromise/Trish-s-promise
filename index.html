About Us

Why Trish's Promise exists

Trish's Promise was created to help animals who need another chance. We are building a foster-based rescue focused on safe placement, responsible care, and finding the right home for each animal.

We are currently operating as a small rescue and are not yet recognized as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit organization.
