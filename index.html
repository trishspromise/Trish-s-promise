Our Promise

Helping animals move from uncertainty to safety.

Trish's Promise is a growing animal rescue serving West Virginia. Our goal is to provide safe temporary care, needed veterinary attention, responsible foster placement, and thoughtful adoption matching.

We are currently in the process of formally organizing as a nonprofit and pursuing federal tax-exempt recognition. Until that process is complete, contributions should not be represented as tax-deductible.
