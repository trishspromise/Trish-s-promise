Our Promise

The heart behind Trish's Promise.

Trish's Promise was created in honor of Trish and the love she shared with animals. Her connection with them is part of the heart behind the rescue and the promise to give animals in need another chance.

We are building a rescue rooted in compassion, responsible placement, organized care, and support for the foster and adoptive families who open their homes.

“Kindness, safety, and a second chance can change an animal's whole life.”

Nonprofit status

Trish's Promise is currently in the process of formally organizing as a nonprofit and pursuing federal tax-exempt recognition. Until that process is complete, contributions should not be represented as tax-deductible.
